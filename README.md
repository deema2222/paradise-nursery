Paradise Nursery

Welcome to Paradise Nursery!

Paradise Nursery is an online houseplants shopping website that provides a variety of beautiful indoor plants.

Project Description

The website allows users to browse different categories of houseplants, view product details, and purchase their favorite plants through a shopping cart.

Main Features

Browse different houseplant categories.

View plant images, names, and prices.

Add plants to the shopping cart.

Increase or decrease product quantities.

Remove products from the cart.

View the total shopping cart price.
